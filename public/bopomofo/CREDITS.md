# Bopomofo sound and stroke assets

Source: Ministry of Education, R.O.C. (Taiwan), The Manual of the Phonetic Symbols of Mandarin Chinese, digital edition (2017).
https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/files/site_content/M0001/juyin/

Individual stroke XML/SVG and audio assets in the separately downloadable materials package are offered under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original package:
https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/files/site_content/M0001/juyin/bopomofo_materials_20170213.zip

Hanzi100 uses the 37 unchanged WAV recordings, renamed from F1–F37 to Unicode code points. Each symbol's SVG stroke paths were extracted in stroke order into JSON, without changing their outlines. Hanzi100 renders the paths with its own colors and sizing. This is not a reproduction or adaptation of the manual's text or layout. No endorsement by the Ministry is implied.
